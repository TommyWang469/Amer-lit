# Indigenous Peoples Today Choice Board

I chose the Visual Stories activity about Bunky Echo-Hawk: The Resistance. The video's main message is that Indigenous people are still shaping the world today, and their culture does not have to stay the same to remain meaningful. Echo-Hawk explains that designs people now consider traditional were once new ideas themselves. This helps explain why using modern styles does not mean he is leaving his culture behind. One unexpected connection is his work with Nike's N7 collection, whose sales help fund Native youth programs. His designs bring Indigenous culture into everyday clothing while also helping young people in his community. Another meaningful detail is his use of medicine wheel colors in artwork inspired by fracking near Native lands. By connecting these colors with an environmental problem, he shows that culture is tied to the land and the problems people face there. To me, these examples make the video's argument clear: we need to recognize Indigenous people through the work they are doing now, along with their history. Otherwise, it becomes easy to focus on old images of their culture and overlook what they are trying to protect and change today.

Source: [PBS American Masters, Bunky Echo-Hawk: The Resistance](https://www.pbs.org/video/bunky-echo-hawk-the-resistance-ogx2q9/).
