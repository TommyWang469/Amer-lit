  As a new immigrant, I always ask myself: why do I come to America? In simple words, because of the American dream - everybody has an equal chance to win. Equal chance is the substantial factor of the reason why Americans are willing to challenge themselves no matter how arduous the task is. This leads to all the valuable ideas and works for human progression made by Americans. However, lots of Americans have a work balanced life by cultivating their own interests in downtime. "To be of use" by Marge Piercy is a poem that describes types of people who produce valuable works for the world. For example, Piercy appreciates people who persevere, collaborative and are self-motivated. "Leisure" by W. H. Davis is an ironic poem that satirizes the life which has no downtime for leisure is poor. Author Marge Piercy presents perseverance and committed work as valuable; however W. D. Davis complicates this idea by warning that a life centered only on productivity is empty and stressful when people lose time to appreciate nature.
   In "To be of use", Marge Piercy communicates a key idea of American culture, which is that perseverance creates works that are meaningful to the world. He starts the poem by appreciating people who "jump into work headfirst". "Headfirst" means the people "jump into work" quickly with great energy and enthusiasm. Therefore, it indicates that these people are self-motivated. Because perseverance is used to describe people who have willpower to do work by themselves and improve their works, this is exactly the group of people who are appreciated by the author since this group of people are willing to do work quickly with great enthusiasm. The author also appreciates another group of people "who do what has to be done, again and again". "Again and again" is a straightforward quote that indicates these people are able to master their work repeatedly. Perseverance is directly related to this group of people because perseverance means people are willing to do work repeatedly until the work reaches the level they expect. Finally author Piercy addresses how work is meaningful for the world by stating that, "Hopi vases that held corn, are put in the museum." These "hopi vases", ancient clay jars used by Native Americans to store and carry liquids, are originally made to use. Because they are well made, they becomes art and put into the museum. Thus this indicates that works are meaningful because if people do their work with great perseverance and motivation, these works will be remembered forever like the "hopi vases".
   Although Piercy proposes that Americans do need the sense of tension, Davis reminds us that Americans also need to make time for enjoying nature due to the busy and stressful life they have. This is reinforced by author from using repetitions of "no time". Author repeats "no time" 7 times in the poem and states that people have no time to "stand beneath boughs" and see "streams full of stars". "Boughs" and "stars" are all natural scenarios. Author uses repetition of "no time" and all of these natural scenarios to indicate lack of time in nature. At the end of the poem, author displays life is "a poor life if, full of care/ We have no time to stand and stare". The quote can be interpreted in two parts. First author directly state the type of life is "poor". Secondly, author define the poor life is "full of care", means people need to take care of life in every moment and live in that stressful environment. Also the poor life is that people have "no time to stand and stare", which is exactly what the author mentioned earlier, people have no time to stand and see all these natural scenarios. As a result this type of life being described is refused by the author because he describes it as "poor". In other word, author Davis believes people need to make time for seeing natural scenarios and experiencing nature.
   Together, the poems suggest that Americans should value both meaningful work and the ability to experience life. This can be seen when self-motivated and persevering people contribute useful work to the world. Also, another equally important point, people also need to experience life, can be seen when author W. D. Davis categorizes the life with no time to see beauties of nature as poor life. In world today, significance of downtime is emphasized by researchers Patricia Albulescu and Irina Macsinga in their "Give me a break! A systematic review and meta-analysis on the efficacy of Micro-breaks for increasing well-being and performance" on August 21, 2022. They study effects of short breaks on people's performance of work. They found out that downtime help people to perform better in work afterward by reducing fatigue, and longer break improves more. Instead of seeing downtime as waste of time, it actually supports people's long term perseverance and meaningful work. Overall, challenging ourselves and consistent working are still important; however, people are not machines, so people also need some downtime.
