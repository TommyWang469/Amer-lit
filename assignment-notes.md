## Your story plan

Arthur, the god of the sky, fights an evil god, loses his power, and falls to the ground. He wakes without remembering who he is. A lion, bear, and dog help him even though they have never seen a creature like him. He lives with them for years, recovers, and feels grateful. He creates Earth for the animals and sends his son there as the first human.

The lion brings food. The bear builds shelter. The dog is loyal, stays with Arthur, and takes care of him. The main ideas are the connection between humans and animals, helping others, and working together.

The handwritten selections include cosmological and sociological functions; helpful creatures, the trickster, and a sky god; a journey, magic, separation between Earth and sky, and sacred food.

## Assignment requirements

- Original creation story explaining the origin of the world; work with a partner.
- 500–750 words.
- At least one function of mythology.
- At least two character archetypes and two plot archetypes.
- First written in class on paper without AI assistance. Limited translation tools are allowed.
- Student's own original creative work.
- Text and visuals in Google Slides for reading the entire story aloud to classmates.
- Individual contributions visible in the Google document and paper brainstorming.
- Google document submitted to turnitin.com for grading.

The rubric emphasizes a clear setting and conflict, a logical beginning/middle/end, controlled pacing, developed characters, dialogue and specific details, correct grammar, and participation in every stage. It states that use of AI or other online tools can lead to deductions for failing to create original work.
